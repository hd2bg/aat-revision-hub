Essential accounting formulas

Quick reference for common accounting calculations.

Gross profit

Sales - Cost of sales

The profit made before operating expenses.

Net profit

Gross profit - Expenses

Profit remaining after expenses.

Accounting equation

Assets = Capital + Liabilities

The foundation of double-entry bookkeeping.

Gross profit margin

(Gross profit / Revenue) x 100

Gross profit as a percentage of sales.

Current ratio

Current assets / Current liabilities

Measures short-term liquidity.

Straight-line depreciation

(Cost - Residual value) / Useful life

Annual depreciation expense.

Markup percentage

(Profit / Cost) x 100

Profit measured against cost.

Net profit margin

(Net profit / Revenue) x 100

Net profit as a percentage of sales.
